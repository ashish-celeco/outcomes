06 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Make it human.
Make it coherent.
Make it useful.

Use space deliberately. Let the typography do some of the work. Show real people. Make every claim traceable. Use motion to show depth and change. Let ambient motion settle, use scroll and interaction for movement, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Give every element a reason to be here.
