01 / THE IDEA

Capable enough to build it.
Human enough to care.

Outcomes helps companies redesign how work gets done with AI. We start with the business, build around its people, and stay close as the systems become part of everyday work.

THE PROMISE

AI transformation.
Measured in outcomes.

Clear about the work.
Accountable for what changes.

Sound like someone
you would build with.

Warm, direct, and specific. Explain what we do, who it helps, and what happens next. Have a point of view. Make room for a conversation.

Warm
“Bring a workflow you wish worked better.”
Concrete
“We connect your tools and company knowledge.”
Accountable
“We agree what better looks like, then check.”
WRITE THIS WAY

Describe the work. Use a clear verb. Keep the promise proportional to the evidence. Let useful details carry the story.

LEAVE THIS OUT

“Unlock limitless potential.” “Revolutionise your future.” Generic claims, invented proof, and language that could belong to anyone.

Scale with care.

Start at 120 px wide for the wordmark and 24 px for the compact mark. Keep both readable; use a plain background at small sizes.

outcomes.o.

Keep the character.

Use the supplied artwork. Keep the proportions and dot intact. Don’t add a descriptor, stretch the lettering, or put the mark inside a decorative badge.

On dark surfaces: paper lettering, terracotta dot. In one colour: render the entire mark in forest or paper.

03 / COLOUR

Grounded.
With a little possibility.

Forest and paper do most of the work. Citron is the invitation. Terracotta adds the warmth of something made by hand.

ForestPaperCitron

Select a swatch to copy its hex value.

Text needs a quieter accent. Use rust for coloured text on paper. Terracotta is for marks, objects, and decorative details, where it does not need to carry small text.

Surfaces can shift. Sand #e8dfc9, sage #dce6df, and lavender #e8e3f0 belong to the workbench illustrations. Keep new surface colours muted and let the core palette hold the identity together.
