Privacy & trust.

A useful partnership starts with a clear understanding of how information is handled.

On this website

This website serves its fonts and artwork locally. It does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or a form that collects personal information. The site’s hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and secure the website.

When you book a call

Our booking buttons open the founding team’s existing Google Calendar booking page. Any information you submit there is handled through that service. Review the information on the booking page and Google’s privacy policy before submitting.

Working together

Outcomes Deployment Company is the next chapter of Celeco Labs. For the company’s existing approach to data access, security, and AI governance, read Celeco Labs’ privacy and trust information. Engagement-specific requirements should be discussed and agreed with the team.

Questions?

You can speak with the founding team about privacy, access, or governance before beginning an engagement.
