THE BEHAVIOUR IS THE BRAND

01

Ask at the right moment.

No microphone prompt on arrival. The visitor chooses voice, typing, or the calendar.

02

Show your working.

Names, contact details, and meeting choices appear visibly and stay editable.

03

Confirm before acting.

The agent can find a time. Only the visitor can approve the booking.

04

Leave a human door open.

Direct booking and a handoff remain available when voice is not the right route.
